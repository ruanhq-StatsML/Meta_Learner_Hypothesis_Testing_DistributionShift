\section{AUUC integral}
Our score: $ \mathrm{AUUC} = \frac{1}{n}\int_0^1 g(f)\,df $ where $g(f)$ is cumulative gain
at population fraction $f$ after sorting by $\hat\tau$:
$g_k = (\bar Y_{T,k}-\bar Y_{C,k})(n_{T,k}+n_{C,k})$.
Matches \texttt{sklift.metrics.uplift\_auc\_score} up to normalization (see \texttt{integral\_check}).
