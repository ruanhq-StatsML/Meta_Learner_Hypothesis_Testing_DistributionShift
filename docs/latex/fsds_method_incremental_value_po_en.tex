\begin{abstract}
\textbf{Full Distribution Shift (FSDS) monitoring} in this repository is one REF/LIVE statistical spine reused across uplift fulfillment, agent scheduling, multi-agent debate, LangGraph checkpoints, and federated vertical blocks.
This position paper is organized for executive and technical review in three layers:
\textbf{(i)}~\emph{where} the method runs and what observables $(X,Y,T,W,Z)$ mean in each domain;
\textbf{(ii)}~\emph{what incremental value} is produced at the execution step (not at diagnostic gates alone);
\textbf{(iii)}~\emph{attached result tables} regenerated from checked-in JSON (Tables~R1--R6).
Incremental value means a measured delta vs an explicit baseline---uniform SoT, uniform debate swarm, uncapped treatment spend, or centralized federated matrix---logged on \texttt{impact\_receipt} with \texttt{rule\_ids} for finance reconciliation.
\end{abstract}

\section{Executive summary: method placement and increments}

\paragraph{One method, six surfaces.}
FSDS always compares a \textbf{reference window} to a \textbf{live window} on covariates $X$ (and, in uplift, outcomes $Y$ for concept gates).
The \textbf{same} localization stack (MMD, overlap, DRPerm, LOCO--AUUC, business rules) applies to marketing uplift; credit fulfillment reuses uplift with relabeled $Y$ and economics units.

\paragraph{Where incremental dollars or quality appear.}
Monitoring steps (G0--G3, L1) \emph{localize}; they do not by themselves change spend.
\textbf{Incremental value} first appears when a policy executes:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{Uplift Track A:} rule step \textbf{R} --- REALLOCATE cap (\texttt{econ\_bucket=opex}).
  \item \textbf{Agent Track B--D:} branch/role budgets, debate early-stop, LangGraph partial replay.
  \item \textbf{Federated Track E:} reduced uplink (Eff $\times$); strategic block OFS is scenario-valued separately.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Headline increments (demo artifacts).}
Agent SoT $\sim$34\% lower \$/episode; multi-agent FSDS $\sim$39\% \$/debate with +2pp success; debate early-stop $\sim$20\% round cost; uplift bench OPEX net \textbf{\$3{,}271} across four scenarios (sim treat economics); LangGraph receipt $\sim\$0.019$/episode.

\section{Per-domain formulation and incremental justify}

\subsection{Track A --- Two-batch uplift and credit fulfillment}

\paragraph{Method use.}
Train $\hat\tau(X)$ on REF; score LIVE with frozen $\hat\tau$; run gates G0--G3 and localization L1 on $(X,Y,T,W)$; emit caps at step R before scheduling full $\hat\tau$ relearn (CAPEX ticket).

\paragraph{Objects.}
$X\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times p}$ customer/borrower covariates; $Y\in\{0,1\}$ conversion or default; $T\in\{0,1\}$ treatment arm; $W$ indicates REF vs LIVE row pool.

\paragraph{Incremental value justify.}
Table~\ref{tab:uplift-scenarios} shows four benchmark scenarios.
\textbf{Direct OPEX increment} is the sum of \texttt{estimated\_opex\_net\_usd} on REALLOCATE rules (Hillstrom \$546; synthetic\_covariate \$2{,}268.50; synthetic\_concept \$456.31).
\textbf{Zero OPEX} on hillstrom\_covariate\_drift in this bench run reflects rule tagging (caps not economically tagged), not absence of monitoring.
\textbf{Incremental vs naive baseline:} ``retrain on every alert'' spends CAPEX (\$2{,}500 tickets $\times$4 scenarios in bench) without guaranteed OPEX cap; FSDS path emits OPEX caps first when PO accepts and quintile AUUC fails locally.

\paragraph{Credit.}
Same code; replace \texttt{treatment\_cost\_usd} with expected loss per marginal approval and \texttt{margin\_usd\_per\_conversion} with NIM+fees (see \texttt{fsds\_treasury\_credit\_risk\_po.tex}).

\subsection{Track B --- Agent Skeleton-of-Thought}

\paragraph{Method use.}
\texttt{covariate\_attribution} on episode \texttt{trace\_features} ($X$) between REF and LIVE batches; \texttt{allocate\_branch\_budgets} on segment embeddings $Z$.

\paragraph{Incremental value justify.}
Measured variable cost \$0.0356 $\rightarrow$ \$0.0236 per episode ($\sim$33.6\%) from enumerated token/check budgets (boss pack).
Eq.~\eqref{eq:netgain} splits quality uplift vs variable cost savings when MC success is reported.

\subsection{Track C --- Multi-agent debate}

\paragraph{Method use.}
Three-role embeddings $Z$; LIVE shift on \texttt{con}; \texttt{fit\_agent\_plan} reallocates per-role $L$ and tier; optional \texttt{debate\_early\_stop\_round}.

\paragraph{Incremental value justify.}
Table~\ref{tab:agent-mc}: cost 0.02064 $\rightarrow$ 0.01252 USD/debate (39.3\%); success 0.66 $\rightarrow$ 0.68.
Early-stop adds $\sim$20\% round-level savings (partially stackable with role budgets).

\subsection{Track D --- LangGraph checkpoints}

\paragraph{Method use.}
Node-level traces form $X$; drift injected on \texttt{tool\_call}; intervention attaches \texttt{impact\_receipt} for conditional retry.

\paragraph{Incremental value justify.}
Receipt \texttt{estimated\_net\_gain\_usd} per episode (Table~\ref{tab:agent-mc}); mechanism is partial graph execution vs full replay (CAPEX).

\subsection{Track E --- Federated vertical blocks}

\paragraph{Method use.}
Each silo runs local FSDS on $X_k$ only; server merges payloads under FDR.

\paragraph{Incremental value justify.}
Table~\ref{tab:federated-legal}: legal block MMD$^2=0.041$ vs finance $0.0037$ enables \textbf{targeted} action.
Protocol bench (companion \texttt{federated\_fsds\_protocol\_results.tex}) reports Eff up to \textbf{1410$\times$} vs centralized matrix on Spambase.
Strategic \$125k/quarter unlock in legal demo is \textbf{not} additive to token saves without signed scenario.

\section{Agent economics (incremental decomposition)}

When both cost and success are simulated:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:netgain}
\mathrm{net\_gain}
= (p - p_0)\, v_{\mathrm{succ}}
+ (C^{\mathrm{base}}_{\mathrm{var}} - C^{\mathrm{fsds}}_{\mathrm{var}})
- h_{\mathrm{fsds}}.
\end{equation}
Treasury should map the second term to \textbf{retained} vendor debits and the first to lagged \textbf{cash\_in} (\texttt{bank\_cash\_bridge.py}).
