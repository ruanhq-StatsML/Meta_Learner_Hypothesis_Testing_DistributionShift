Requirements for $Z$ (Segments) vs $X$ (Batch Traces)}

\begin{abstract}
FSDS agent integration uses \textbf{two embedding layers}: segment matrix $Z\in\mathbb{R}^{B\times d}$ drives \emph{within-episode} budgets, while episode vectors $X\in\mathbb{R}^{p}$ (built from $\mathrm{mean}(Z)$ plus scalars) drive \emph{REF vs LIVE} batch monitoring.
This note specifies production requirements for ReAct and multi-agent debate---the patterns called out as most practical in the full scenario PO.
\end{abstract}

\section{Two layers (do not merge)}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
Object & Shape & Consumer & Role \\
\midrule
$Z$ & $(B,d)$ & \texttt{allocate\_branch\_budgets} & Per-segment shift, $L_b$, tier, checks \\
$X$ & $(n,p)$ batches & \texttt{covariate\_attribution} & MMD$^2$, domain AUC, VIMP on $W$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\textbf{Requirement E0:} Every episode logs both $Z$ and one row of $X$; sidecar stacks rows into $X_{\mathrm{ref}}$, $X_{\mathrm{live}}$.

\section{Building $Z$}
Each row is L2-normalized (\texttt{\_normalize\_rows} in \texttt{applications.py}):
\[
\tilde{Z}_b = \frac{Z_b}{\|Z_b\|_2+\epsilon}.
\]
\paragraph{ReAct ($B=3$).} Rows: thought, action, observation embeddings (dim $d$ fixed across production).
\paragraph{Multi-agent ($B=3$).} Rows: pro, con, judge \emph{utterance} embeddings; LIVE demos inject drift on con.

\section{Building $X$ from $Z$}
Pattern-specific \texttt{trace\_features} (concatenation contract):
\begin{align*}
X_{\mathrm{ep}} &= \big[\;\mathrm{mean}_b(\tilde{Z}_b)\;;\;\text{scalars}\;\big] \in \mathbb{R}^{p}, \\
\text{scalars}_{\mathrm{ReAct}} &= [\;B,\;\mathrm{mean}(q),\;\min(q)\;], \\
\text{scalars}_{\mathrm{MA}} &= [\;B,\;\mathrm{std}(\tilde{Z}),\;0\;].
\end{align*}
\textbf{Requirements E1--E3:} Fixed $p$ per pattern; same encoder for REF/LIVE; no LIVE-fitted normalization leaking into REF.

\section{Within-episode shift scores}
Reference centroid $c=\mathrm{mean}_b(\tilde{Z}_b)$,
\[
s_b = \frac{\|\tilde{Z}_b-c\|_2}{\max_{b'}\|\tilde{Z}_{b'}-c\|_2}.
\]
Budget (see \texttt{budget.py}):
\[
L_b = \mathrm{clip}(\kappa\, s_b\, q_b\, \mathrm{need}_b,\, L_{\min,b},\, C_{\mathrm{lat}}).
\]
ReAct: high $s_{\mathrm{obs}}$ $\Rightarrow$ more checks on observation, trim thought/action $L_b$.
Multi-agent: high $s_{\mathrm{con}}$ $\Rightarrow$ smaller tier on con; judge keeps floor tokens (Table~R10b in full PO).

\section{Multi-agent: two tensor contracts}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{Micro (FSDS):} $Z\in\mathbb{R}^{3\times d}$ for the round you will execute; \texttt{fit\_agent\_plan}.
  \item \textbf{Macro (early-stop):} $E\in\mathbb{R}^{R\times B\times d}$ round--role embeddings; \texttt{debate\_early\_stop\_round} uses inter-round centroid dispersion.
\end{itemize}
Do not feed $(R,B,d)$ into \texttt{fit\_plan} as $Z$; roles for budgeting are the current-round $(B,d)$ slice.

\section{Production checklist}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Fixed encoder, dimension $d$; store $\tilde{Z}$ and $X_{\mathrm{ep}}$ per episode.
  \item Rolling REF/LIVE windows of $X$; hourly/daily \texttt{fit\_agent\_plan}.
  \item Debate: log $E$ each round; run early-stop before round $r{+}1$.
  \item Validate: inject drift on one row of $Z$ only $\Rightarrow$ argmax$_b s_b$ matches; $X$ batches show MMD$^2>0$.
\end{enumerate}
