\section{The skill}
The skill is \texttt{rap\_clever\_covariate\_guide}.
One beat is one committed search step.
During the episode the beat carries the state \(X\) and the action prior \(e\), the stable score the search assigned to the committed step, clipped to \((0.001,0.999)\).
It does not carry \(Y\).
\(Y=1\) if the episode's search returns a solved state, and \(Y=0\) otherwise.
\(Y\) is written onto every committed step only after the search returns, so the step that produced \(Y\) cannot read its own outcome.
The next episode can, because its window holds only beats whose \(Y\) is already known.
After that write-back the clever covariate is
\[
H=\frac{Y-e}{e(1-e)},
\]
the same influence-function weight the doubly robust pseudo-outcome learner uses to re-weight a residual.
The skill renders \(H\) as one line of natural language and prepends it to the planning prompt, so the model that proposes the next actions reads the anomaly.
The online anomaly score is \(|H|\).
The sign is the adjustment: \(H>\tau\) is aggressive, \(H<-\tau\) is conservative, and the remainder is keep.

For \(Y\in\{0,1\}\) the two values are immediate.
A success gives \(H=1/e>0\); a failure gives \(H=-1/(1-e)<0\).
If \(e<1/2\) then \(1/e>1/(1-e)\), so a confident success has the larger magnitude.
The threshold \(\tau\) is therefore a band on the prior, not a free constant: a success fires aggressive iff \(e<1/\tau\), a failure fires conservative iff \(e>1-1/\tau\).
Because \(|H|\ge 1\) for every binary beat, any \(\tau<1\) never returns keep and the sign fires on every finished episode; a \(\tau>1\) opens a dead zone around \(e=1/2\) where an outcome that matched a middling prior is kept.
For a soft \(Y\in[0,1]\) the covariate is continuous and \(\tau\) is calibrated on a no-drift reference window at a quantile of \(|H|\), the same null-calibration the repository already uses for its permutation \(p\)-value.
Two identities pin down the channels.
The conservative share equals the finished-failure rate \(1-\bar Y\); it is a mirror of the outcome.
The mean \(|H|\) mixes a large success weight \(1/e\) with a small failure weight \(1/(1-e)\), so when the prior sits below one half the magnitude falls as the outcome falls.
The channels free to move with the read are the activation rate \(\bar Y\) and the prior of the chosen action.

\section{Reading the sign on three one-step searches}
Each search has three offers and keeps the top one.
Forty users, drift at user 16, twenty-four users after the drift.
Activations are fixed modular functions of the user index, so every number below is reconstructable and seed-free.
A judge commits one offer per user; before the drift it commits the habit-shaped offer, and after the drift the mix flips it to a low-yield offer.
The sign-not-read policy always follows the judge.
The sign-read policy follows the judge through a warmup, then switches once, permanently, to the highest stable-score offer when a drift trigger fires.

\midrule

Offer     & sign read     & 0.500 & 0.625 & \(+11\) & \(+3\) \\
\addlinespace

\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The offer search is the validation target: on the unread stream the prior on the committed offer moves from \(0.50\) to \(0.20\) and \(Y\) falls to \(0.167\); the sign matches \(Y\) on every post-drift step, so \(|H|\) moves only from \(2.00\) to \(1.875\) (a success carries \(1/0.20=5.00\), a failure \(1/0.80=1.25\)) and the conservative share rises from \(0.500\) to \(0.833\).
Reading the sign commits the growth offer and lifts \(Y\) to \(0.625\).
Triage is the bounded case: the best available branch clears only \(0.55\), so the read returns \(Y\) to the baseline and no higher.
Retrieval is the strong case: the deep branch clears \(0.75\), so the read lifts \(Y\) six activations above the baseline.

\section{The online RF permutation test that fires the re-route}
The last-four-loss margin used above is ad hoc.
The re-route can instead be fired by the repository's own machinery: an online RandomForest permutation two-sample test.
At each step the reference batch is the first eight finished beats and the recent batch is the last six; the features are the clever-covariate triple \([e,H,|H|]\); the statistic is the forest's out-of-bag accuracy at separating the two batches; and the null is 80 label permutations, so
\[
p=\frac{1+\#\{\text{permuted accuracy}\ge\text{observed}\}}{1+80},
\qquad p_{\min}=\tfrac{1}{81}=0.0123 .
\]
A rolling mean and rolling standard deviation of \(Y\) over the last four beats are read alongside it.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrrrr}
\toprule
Search & \(p\) at drift & \(p\) final & First \(p<0.05\) & Roll.\ mean \(Y\) & Roll.\ std \(Y\) \\
\midrule
Offer     & 0.531 & 0.012 & \(m=19\) & \(0.500\to0.000\) & \(0.500\to0.000\) \\
Triage    & 0.531 & 0.012 & \(m=20\) & \(0.500\to0.250\) & \(0.500\to0.433\) \\
Retrieval & 0.531 & 0.012 & \(m=19\) & \(0.500\to0.000\) & \(0.500\to0.000\) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
At the drift step \(m=16\) the recent window still holds only pre-drift beats, indistinguishable from the reference, so the forest cannot separate them and \(p\) sits at \(0.531\).
As post-drift beats enter the recent window \(p\) falls, crosses \(0.05\) two-to-four beats after the drift, and reaches the permutation floor \(0.0123\) by \(m=40\).
The three searches share the same pre-drift stream, so the drift-step \(p\) and the final \(p\) are identical; only the crossing time differs, and triage crosses one beat later because its post-drift shortfall is milder.
The rolling mean tracks the fall in \(Y\); in triage the post-drift outcomes are mixed, so the rolling standard deviation rises to \(0.433\) and is itself a drift signal that the two silent searches do not show.

When the switch is driven by this \(p\)-value instead of the margin rule, the recovery is preserved:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}
\toprule
Search & Margin switch \(\to Y\) & RFPerm switch \(\to Y\) & Extra vs judge \\
\midrule
Offer     & user 17, \(0.625\) & user 19, \(0.583\) & \(+10\) \\
Triage    & user 19, \(0.500\) & user 20, \(0.458\) & \(+5\)  \\
Retrieval & user 17, \(0.750\) & user 19, \(0.667\) & \(+13\) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The permutation test switches two-to-three users later than the margin rule, because it waits until the recent window is post-drift enough to reject at \(0.05\), and it recovers a little less as a result (\(+10\), \(+5\), \(+13\) against the drifted judge, versus \(+11\), \(+6\), \(+15\)).
That is the deliberate trade: a calibrated false-positive rate in exchange for a few beats of latency.
The recovery still stands on all three searches, and it is now fired by the same distribution-shift test the repository is built around, not by a hand-tuned loss margin.

\section{What the read does not do}
The recovered \(Y\) equals the realized activation of the switched branch over the post-drift window, not its stable score.
The read cannot manufacture a branch that does not exist, which is the triage ceiling, and it cannot recover a drop when the next step's ranking does not consume the sign.
That last boundary is the Game-of-24 control in the companion note: the same \(H\), the same window, and the same anomaly score are present, the argmax still follows the frozen forest, and \(Y\) falls from \(1.00\) to \(0.25\).
The permutation \(p\)-value would still fire on that stream, but with no better branch to route toward the fired re-route buys nothing; detection and remedy are separate, and the read supplies only the remedy.

\section{The production value}
The skill is a prompt plus one clipped division per step, and the trigger is a test the codebase already ships.
Its value is three edges.
First, the sign is a routing signal: on the offer, triage, and retrieval searches it recovers 11, 6, and 15 of the 24 post-drift activations under the margin rule, and 10, 5, and 13 under the permutation trigger, the business rate a drifted judge silently forfeits.
Second, the search cost is flat: the number of children scored per step does not change with the read, so the recovered activations cost no extra expansion, and the wasted spend on a drifted branch is cut a handful of beats after the drift.
Third, detection and steering share one pipeline: the online RandomForest permutation \(p\)-value that fires the re-route is the same distribution-shift statistic this repository permutes elsewhere, and the rolling mean and standard deviation are the monitoring read, so the anomaly stream that steers the prompt is also the stream that raises the drift alert.
The read is complementary to a numeric UCB term: the number steers the tree statistics, and the sign steers the language that proposes the next branch.
The honest scope is the boundary above: the read pays when a better branch exists and the ranking consumes the sign, it recovers only the shortfall when the best branch is weak, and the permutation trigger trades a few beats of latency for a controlled false-positive rate.
